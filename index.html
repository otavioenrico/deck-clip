Privacy policy

Deck Clip runs entirely on your computer. There’s no server, no account, no data collection and no usage tracking. Nothing you copy, download or pin is ever sent to Deck Labs or anyone else.

What you copied, your downloads and your pinned files are read only to show in the panel. A site only receives the file you click, and only in the field you used. Sites never see your download list, your pinned files or what you copied.

Pinned files are kept inside the extension, on your computer. Settings are kept in Chrome and, if Chrome sync is on, Chrome itself carries them to your other computers. Uninstalling Deck Clip deletes everything.

Permissions: site access (to replace the file picker), clipboard (to show what you copied), downloads (to list recent ones) and storage (for settings and pinned files).

Política de privacidade

O Deck Clip funciona inteiro no seu computador. Não tem servidor, conta, coleta de dados nem análise de uso. Nada do que você copia, baixa ou fixa é enviado para a Deck Labs ou para qualquer outra pessoa.

Permissões: acesso aos sites (para trocar o seletor de arquivos), área de transferência (para mostrar o que você copiou), downloads (para listar os recentes) e armazenamento (para ajustes e fixados).
